Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

StampBook, operated by Mandal Industries, is free to use. The only paid product is the optional ad-free plan (weekly, monthly or yearly). This policy applies to those purchases.

1. Refund window

You may request a full refund within 7 days of purchase if you were charged by mistake, the ad-free plan did not activate, or a technical problem prevented its use.

2. Not refundable

  • Requests made after 7 days.
  • Accounts suspended for violating our Terms.

3. How to request

Email stampbook03@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 90837 87933 with your registered phone/email, the payment date, amount and transaction ID. We respond within 3 to 5 business days.

4. Processing time

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and typically take 5 to 10 business days to appear.

5. Cancellation

Ad-free plans are one-time purchases for the chosen period and do not auto-renew. Cancelling is therefore not required, and ads return when the period ends.

6. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above.
