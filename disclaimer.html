StampBook is a billing and invoicing app operated by Mandal Industries. This page explains what our software and our guides can and cannot do.

1. General information only

The articles in our blog and the text on this website are general information for small business owners. They are not legal, tax, accounting or financial advice. Tax rules, including GST, change over time. Please confirm details with a chartered accountant, a tax professional or the official government portal before you act on them.

2. You choose the tax details

StampBook calculates totals from the tax rate, discount and amounts that you enter. It does not decide which GST rate, HSN or SAC code, or tax treatment applies to what you sell. You are responsible for entering the correct details and for the accuracy of every invoice you issue.

3. Not an accounting or tax filing tool

StampBook helps you create invoices, track clients and payments, and view simple reports. It does not file GST returns, and it does not replace your books of account or advice from your accountant.

4. Payments

Payments go directly to your own UPI ID or your own payment gateway account. StampBook does not hold your funds and is not a party to the sale between you and your client. Payment failures or delays caused by banks, UPI apps or gateways are outside our control.

5. Availability

We work to keep the app running, but we cannot promise it will always be available or free of errors. Please keep your own copies of important invoices, for example by downloading the PDF.

StampBook is free to use and shows ads served by Google AdSense, with an ad-free plan available. We do not control advertisers or the content of external websites we link to, and a link does not mean we endorse that site. See our Privacy Policy for how advertising cookies are handled.
